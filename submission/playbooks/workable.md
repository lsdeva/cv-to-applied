# Workable (apply.workable.com)

- Account needed: no
- Typical flow: `/j/<code>/apply/` single page -> Submit application -> `?success` "Thank you! Your application has been submitted successfully." The copy email from candidates.workablemail.com does not include the answers.

## Field mechanics
- Phone has a country flag button next to `[name=phone]`. Type the country into its search.
- Custom dropdowns `#input_CA_<n>_input` open a `[role=option]` list, and typing filters it.
